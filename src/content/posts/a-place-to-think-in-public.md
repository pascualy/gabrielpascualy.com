I wanted a home for my writing: a place to develop an idea beyond a short post, keep useful observations together, and connect with people working on similar questions.

I lead product at [Ketryx](https://www.ketryx.com/), where we build AI for teams developing regulated software. That work sits at the intersection of product, engineering, and quality.

Here, I plan to write about the decisions behind useful AI products: how people review outputs, how software fits into existing workflows, and how teams decide what they can rely on.

I’ll also share working notes on product judgment and things I’m learning along the way.

If you’re exploring similar questions, you can find me on [Twitter / X](https://x.com/gpascualy).
